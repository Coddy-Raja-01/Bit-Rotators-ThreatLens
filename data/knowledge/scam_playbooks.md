# Scam Playbooks and Social Engineering Modus Operandi

## 1. Impersonation of Financial Institutions and KYC Scams
Attackers send urgent SMS or WhatsApp messages claiming the recipient's bank account, debit card, or SIM card is blocked due to pending KYC (Know Your Customer) verification. The message directs the victim to an impersonated portal or instructs them to call a fraudulent helpline. Once on the site, the victim is prompted to enter net banking credentials, card numbers, CVV, and OTPs.

## 2. Part-Time Remote Job and Task-Based Rating Scams
Victims are contacted via Telegram or WhatsApp with promises of high daily earnings for rating hotel listings, liking YouTube videos, or completing app reviews. Initially, small incentive payouts ($10–$50) are credited to build confidence. The victim is then coerced into depositing cryptocurrency or bank transfers into "prepaid VIP merchant accounts" to unlock higher commissions, after which withdrawals are frozen.

## 3. Electricity and Utility Disconnection Extortion
Fraudulent notifications state that electricity or water services will be disconnected tonight at 9:30 PM due to unpaid bills. The message provides a fraudulent official contact number. When contacted, attackers instruct victims to install remote desktop utilities (AnyDesk, QuickSupport) to process a nominal $1 verification transaction, allowing the attacker to take over the device.

## 4. Crypto Giveaway Multiplier and Seed Phrase Extraction
Fraudulent cryptocurrency portals claim to offer automated double-your-crypto events sponsored by prominent figures. Other variants mimic decentralized wallet interfaces (MetaMask, Phantom) warning of account suspension unless the user enters their 12 or 24-word secret recovery phrase.
